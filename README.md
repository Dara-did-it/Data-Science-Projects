This project analysed airline delay causes using PySpark and other Python libraries. The goal was to conduct exploratory data analysis to identify trends in flight delays, determine which airlines have the highest percentage of delayed flights, highlight those with the most carrier-caused delays, pinpoint airports with the highest delay rates, understand the main causes of delays, examine seasonal variations, and identify months with the highest and lowest delay percentages to reveal seasonality.



By analysing delay causes and patterns, the project provides actionable insights that can improve operational efficiency. Identifying airlines with high delay rates and dominant carrier-caused delays allows carriers (such as SkyWest Airlines Inc. and Southwest Airlines Co., which had the highest total carrier-caused delays) to investigate internal processes, resource allocation (crews, aircraft maintenance), and scheduling. Addressing these issues can reduce operational costs and enhance customer satisfaction.



Identifying airports with high delay rates, such as Youngstown/Warren, Ohio, can prompt airport authorities to assess capacity, air traffic control systems, ground handling, and runway efficiency. These insights support strategic investments and operational changes to reduce congestion and delays.



By understanding the primary causes of delays, such as Late Aircraft, NAS, and Carrier delays, policymakers can develop targeted strategies and policies. These may include technology investments, improved air traffic management, or stronger airline operational standards.
